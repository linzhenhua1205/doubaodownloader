## 服务器行业技术观点

- **NVIDIA 自研 HBM（NVHBM）vs 依赖内存厂供应**：正方（TrendForce, https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/08/27/news-nvidias-supply-commitments-soar-to-279b-as-memory-costs-surge-new-nvhbm-boosts-bandwidth-30-cuts-power-15/）——$279B 承诺+NVHBM 定制（带宽+30%/功耗-15%）表明"定制+锁量"双管齐下，垂直整合是确定方向；反方/风险（TrendForce 同文）——CFO 预警内存与组件成本压毛利（75% 且下行），NVHBM 量产依赖晶圆厂/HBM 厂排期，短期难解短缺；辨析：本库判断 NVHBM 属"设计定制"非"制造自研"，2026-2028 内存卖方市场格局难改
- **HBF 高带宽闪存能否成为 HBM 容量平替**：正方（Oxmiq Labs, https://www.servethehome.com/oxmiq-labs-hbf-in-ai-compute-at-hot-chips-2026/）——带宽 0.384→3.072TB/s、UCIe 8→32GT/s、16-high 512GiB，同成本容量为 HBM 8-16 倍，适合推理容量层；反方/风险（Tom's Hardware, https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ssds/hot-chips-2026-high-bandwidth-flash-promises-massive-bandwidth-and-capacity-but-its-usability-is-extremely-limited-new-memory-format-strikes-a-balance-between-hbm-and-nand-flash）——"usability extremely limited"，介乎 HBM 与 NAND 的折中，生态/标准未立；辨析：本库判断短期定位 niche 容量层，需观察 UCIe 生态适配与闪存厂跟进
- **定制 ASIC（Jalapeño）挑战通用 GPU**：正方（Tom's Hardware, 2026-08-27, https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/hot-chips-2026-openais-jalapeno-ai-asic-unpacked-accelerator-developed-using-ai-achieves-efficiency-and-throughput-gains-against-power-hungry-blackwell）——9 个月开发、reticle 级、AI 辅助设计，推理效率/吞吐优于 Blackwell；反方/风险（STH 评论生态, https://www.servethehome.com/openai-jalapeno-asic-at-hot-chips-2026/；TrendForce, https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/08/26/news-openai-debuts-jalapeno-ai-inference-chip-with-samsung-reportedly-supplying-hbm4/）——ASIC 固化单一负载、迭代周期长、CUDA 生态不可替代，且依赖 Broadcom 产能与 Samsung HBM4；辨析：本库判断 ASIC 仅头部玩家推理规模足够大时成立，通用 GPU 仍是训练与长尾主力
